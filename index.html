Our Story

Born From Intention

Manoshia was founded on a singular belief: that luxury and movement are not at odds. That the most beautiful things in life should also be the most functional.

From Lagos to London, our designs speak to a generation of people who refuse to compartmentalise their lives — who demand that their wardrobes keep up with who they are at every moment.

We craft each piece with editorial precision, using performance materials that honour both the body and the eye.

The People Behind Manoshia

Meet the Founders

Adaeze Manoshi

Founder & Creative Director

Adaeze founded Manoshia with a vision to marry editorial luxury with the energy of active life. Former creative lead at a Lagos fashion house.

Kofi Asante

Head of Design

Kofi brings architectural precision to every silhouette. Trained in Milan and Accra, he leads the design language across all Manoshia collections.

Zara Obi

Brand & Communications

Zara shapes how the world sees Manoshia. With a background in luxury brand strategy, she crafts every touchpoint into a statement of intent.
