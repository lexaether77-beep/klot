    Our Story

    Born From Intention

    KLOT was founded on a singular belief: that performance and impact are not at odds. That the most powerful things you wear should also be the most functional.

    Victory through harmony, designed for impact — our pieces speak to a generation of people who refuse to hold back, who demand that their wardrobes keep pace with who they are at every moment.

    We craft each piece with athletic precision, using performance materials that honour both the body and the bold.
